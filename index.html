LinkPlay

Paste a video link. Press play. Nothing is downloaded, nothing is saved.

What plays here: direct video file links (.mp4, .webm) and stream links (.m3u8 HLS, .mpd DASH).
What doesn't: YouTube / watch-page URLs — those aren't direct video links and can't be played by any web player.
If a link won't play: the site hosting the video may block outside players (hotlink protection). Only the video's host can allow it.
